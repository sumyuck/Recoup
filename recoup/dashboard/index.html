The claim Recoup can defend

Recovery dashboards report gross. Recoup reports what it actually caused.

A never-contacted holdout removes money that would return anyway. A merchant baseline removes the value of retries they already run. Every intervention is then policy-gated and written to a hash-chained ledger.

Headline — incremental, not raw

Arm comparison

Every arm shares the same corpus, executor, policy gate and world. Only the decision policy differs, so the delta is attributable to the decision policy. Holdout is never contacted — it measures what recovers with the agent switched off.

False-positive cost

The cost a raw recovery rate hides: recoveries that arrived on their own and must not be claimed, contacts spent on customers who were always going to pay, and the permanent opt-outs that resulted.
